6. Shaft checks

Torque, joint life, length and angle against the selected shaft

TDW applies when the torque reverses, otherwise TDSch = 1.4·TDW6. n·β limits and ncrit are read from the catalogue charts9 (±3 %), or the steel-tube formula10 for tubes not charted; both assume rigid supports near the flanges. Near ½ ncrit the joint's twice-per-revolution excitation can cause vibration11.
