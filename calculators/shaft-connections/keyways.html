F = 2T/D. Shear τ = F/(W·L); bearing σb = F/((H/2)·L)3. Allowable shear = 0.577×Sy/FS; allowable bearing = Sy/FS3,4 — bearing usually governs. A simplified static check: ignores stress concentration, fatigue and shock; keep L within the hub length and treat L > ~2×d with caution4.

Applicable design standards

  • DIN 6885-1:2021 — Drive type fastenings without taper action, parallel keys, keyways — Deep pattern — Part 1: Dimensions, tolerances, mass.
  • ISO/R 773:1969 — Rectangular or square parallel keys and their corresponding keyways (dimensions in millimetres).
  • DIN 6885-3:2021 — Low pattern parallel keys/keyways (shallower t1/t2 for lighter-duty shafts) — not reproduced on this page, which covers deep pattern ("Type A") only.

Doesn't reproduce the standards' full tolerance tables, fillet radii/chamfers, taper/gib-head/Woodruff keys, or splines. Verify safety-critical, high-torque, fatigue or shock-loaded joints against the actual standard.
