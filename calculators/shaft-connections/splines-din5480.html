Applicable design standards

  • DIN 5480-1 — Involute splines based on reference diameters — Part 1: General.
  • DIN 5480-2 — Involute splines based on reference diameters — Part 2: Nominal dimensions and inspection dimensions.

Library data (data/splines_din5480.js) was keyed from the standards on 2026-10-19: 721 preferred sizes, modules 0.5–10, 30° only. Cold-rolled shafts and diameter-centred fits are not included. Verify conformance-critical splines against the standard.

References

  1. Spline Data — DIN 5480 (internal spreadsheet; 60×1.5×38, 85×1.5×55, 60×2.5×22 and 70×1.25×54 versions) — geometry, tolerances, measuring dimensions and drawing-data layout.
  2. Spline Stress Calculations — DIN 5480 (internal spreadsheet) — stress equations, factor and allowable tables. The sheet cites Machinery's Handbook 27th ed., Gears, Splines and Cams (pp. 2169–2174) and D. W. Dudley, When Splines Need Stress Control; neither opened for this page. The sheet applies Le even where the spline is shorter; this page uses the smaller of L and Le.
  3. DIN 5480-1:2006-03 (PDF supplied) — Tables 4, 7, 9; DIN 5480-2:2006-05 (English translation read via scribd.com) — Tables 2–33 preferred sizes, pin diameters DM, teeth measured k and Wk reliability flags. Wk calculated, matching table values to 0.002 mm.
