Where these numbers came from

Directly grounded in the sources below: the Position bands' four boundaries (0.25 mm, 0.1 mm, 0.0127 mm) come straight from published "reasonable vs. too tight" hole-location figures2; the General-tier default used across most form/orientation characteristics (≈0.13–0.4 mm for a sub-300 mm feature) and the Angularity bands come from a CNC shop's own published standard-tolerance table1; the High-precision/Ultra boundary for Position, Circularity, Cylindricity, Perpendicularity and Straightness (roughly 5 µm down to 1–2 µm) comes from a jig-boring shop's stated capability3. Concentricity and Symmetry are set equal to Total Runout and Parallelism respectively, per the common practitioner view that they're rarely toleranced or measured on their own4. Everything else — Flatness, Straightness, Profile of a Line/Surface, Cylindricity and Circular Runout's exact numbers, and every Commercial-tier floor — is this page's own engineering-judgement estimate scaled from the cited anchors, not yet independently sourced per number. Treat the whole scale as indicative until it's checked against your own shop's capability data.

Applicable design standards

  • ASME Y14.5-2018 — Dimensioning and Tolerancing.
  • ISO 1101:2017 — Geometrical product specifications (GPS) — Geometrical tolerancing.
  • ISO 2768-1:1989 / ISO 2768-2:1989 — General tolerances for linear/angular and geometric dimensions without individual tolerance indications.

This page doesn't reproduce those standards' own tolerance grades or verification rules — it's a rough, process-capability-based "how tight is tight" guide, not a substitute for calling out an actual tolerance class.

References

  2. Let's have a discussion on position tolerance of dowel pins — eng-tips.com (Ø0.010″ called a reasonable machining tolerance for hole location; Ø0.001″ reasonable for standard machining; Ø0.0005″ "way too tight" for conventional/CNC milling; Ø0.00025″ impractical).
  3. Tight-Tolerance Jig Bore Machining — criterionprecision.com (jig-boring hole location within 1–5 µm of datum, ultra-precision work to 1–2 µm; bore diameter ±0.0002″; roundness/perpendicularity/straightness under 5 µm for mold-base work).
  4. Concentricity (GD&T) Explained — fractory.com (concentricity described as difficult to achieve and measure; runout ≈ circularity + concentricity when a part is round; recommends runout, position or profile as the practical substitute).
