Assumptions

  • Steady, fully-developed, single-phase incompressible liquid flow in a straight round pipe (no fittings other than the lumped ΣK term).
  • Darcy friction factor: laminar f = 64/Re below Re 2300; Swamee–Jain explicit approximation above Re 4000; linear blend between12.
  • Water and glycol mixes interpolated from tables (linear for density, log-linear for viscosity); oils from the ASTM D341 (Walther) relation fitted to datasheet 40/100 °C viscosities.
  • Elevation change and fittings beyond ΣK are not included — add static head separately if the line isn’t level.

Applicable design standards

  • ASTM D341 — Standard Practice for Viscosity-Temperature Equations and Charts for Liquid Petroleum or Hydrocarbon Products (oil viscosity vs temperature).
  • ISO 3448 — Industrial liquid lubricants — ISO viscosity classification (VG 32 / VG 46 grades).

This page does not reproduce either standard. Conformance-critical work should refer to the standards and to measured data for the actual fluid.
