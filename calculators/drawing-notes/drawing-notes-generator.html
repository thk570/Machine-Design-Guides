Drawing notes block (BS 8888 style) ·

1. Part definition

Notes and types from the Drawing Notes Library1, filtered by material (materials from the shared library2). A template sets the material and replaces the notes; use “Add another…” where a step needs more than one note.

2. Notes and order

Drag ⋮⋮ or use ↑ ↓ to reorder. Values left empty, and options left unpicked, are copied as written (e.g. []).
