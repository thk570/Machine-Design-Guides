Applicable design standards

  • ISO 898-1 — Mechanical properties of fasteners made of carbon steel and alloy steel — Part 1: Bolts, screws and studs with specified property classes
  • ISO 3506-1 — Fasteners — Mechanical properties of corrosion-resistant stainless steel fasteners — Part 1: Bolts, screws and studs with specified grades and property classes
  • ISO 261 / ISO 262 — ISO general purpose metric screw threads — General plan / Selected sizes for screws, bolts and nuts
  • ISO 724 — ISO general-purpose metric screw threads — Basic dimensions
  • ISO 4762 — Hexagon socket head cap screws · ISO 14579 — Hexalobular socket head cap screws
  • ISO 7380-1 — Button head screws — Part 1: Hexagon socket button head screws
  • ISO 10642 — Hexagon socket countersunk head screws · ISO 14581 — Hexalobular socket countersunk flat head screws
  • DIN 7984 — Hexagon socket head cap screws with low head · ISO 14580 — Hexalobular socket cheese head screws
  • ISO 4014 / ISO 4017 — Hexagon head bolts / Hexagon head screws — Product grades A and B
  • ISO 4162 — Hexagon flange bolts — Small series
  • ISO 10664 — Hexalobular internal driving feature for bolts and screws
  • ISO 273 — Fasteners — Clearance holes for bolts and screws · DIN 974-1 — Counterbore diameters for bolts with cylindrical heads · DIN 74 — Countersinks for countersunk head screws · ISO 15065 — Countersinks for countersunk head screws with head configuration in accordance with ISO 7721

This page does not reproduce any standard's own tables. Its values come from publicly available manufacturer and distributor extracts, cross-checked where two sources were available. For conformance-critical work (procurement, inspection, safety-critical joints), check the actual standard.
