A shared material property reference arranged by material type, then grade and condition, so other calculators can pull consistent data. Each row states its basis: an MMPDS design minimum (S, A or B), a specification minimum, or a typical value. Click a row for the full property set, alternative names and sources.

Not a substitute for MMPDS. Values were re-keyed by hand from MMPDS-14 design tables1 and manufacturer datasheets, converted to metric. MMPDS values are shown for the L (longitudinal) direction with the basis stated (S, A or B); the handbook's full allowables, directions and conditions are not reproduced. For certified or safety-critical structure use MMPDS or the material specification itself.

Where a grade's own data gives no value (most often G, ν, density, CTE or melting range), a typical value for the material family is used, shown in italics with its source. CTI values for insulating materials are typical figures per IEC 601122; verify against the grade's datasheet before relying on a material group.

Cost bands are approximate, small-lot UK stock prices from shop listings where one was found, otherwise estimates (shown with ~). They are a relative guide to material and form, not quotes, and are not live.

Applicable design standards

  • IEC 60112 — Method for the determination of the proof and the comparative tracking indices of solid insulating materials.
  • MMPDS-14 — Metallic Materials Properties Development and Standardization.
