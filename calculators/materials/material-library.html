MMPDS-style reference (not MMPDS data) ·

A shared material property reference, arranged the way MMPDS (Metallic Materials Properties Development and Standardization) lays out its allowables — by material type, then grade/condition — so other calculators on this site can pull consistent material data instead of each hard-coding its own. Density, elastic constants (E, G, ν), yield/ultimate strength, thermal expansion, thermal conductivity and melting range, per metallic grade; Comparative Tracking Index (CTI) and IEC 60664-1 material group for insulating materials.

This is not MMPDS. MMPDS (formerly MIL-HDBK-5) is the statistically-derived, paywalled allowables handbook used for aerospace structural substantiation1 — its A/B-basis values come from pooled, controlled test programmes and are not reproduced here. This page instead collects typical room-temperature properties from manufacturer/aggregator datasheets, organised the same way (type → grade → condition), as a quick reference — not a substitute for MMPDS or a material spec when substantiating a design.

Where a value wasn't given for the specific grade/condition (most often Poisson's ratio or shear modulus), a typical value for that material family is used instead and marked italic in the detail view, with its own source. Every other value traces to a grade-specific datasheet — see each grade's references below.

The Comparative Tracking Index (CTI) values under "Insulating Materials" are typical figures for the base polymer/material family from manufacturer and aggregator datasheets, per IEC 601122. Actual CTI depends on the specific formulation, fillers and grade, and can vary within the ranges shown — always verify against the manufacturer's datasheet for the grade actually used before relying on the material group for an insulation-coordination calculation.

Applicable design standards

  • MMPDS — Metallic Materials Properties Development and Standardization, the FAA-sponsored handbook of statistically-based design allowables for metallic aerospace structures (successor to MIL-HDBK-5).
  • IEC 60112 — Method for the determination of the proof and the comparative tracking indices of solid insulating materials.

For safety-critical or certified structure, allowables should come from MMPDS (or the applicable material spec / test programme), not from this page.

References — per-grade sources are listed under each grade below; this page's own text cites only the MMPDS and IEC 60112 standards, above.
