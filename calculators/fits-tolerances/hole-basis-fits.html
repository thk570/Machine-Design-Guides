c–h/C–G are clearance letters, js/JS is symmetric, k/m/n/p/r/s/u are transition/interference2. Hole letters mirror the same-letter shaft deviation (ISO 286-1's Δ correction is 0 for clearance letters, JS, and P–U); K, M and N use a grade-differential Δ correction instead (Δ = ITn−ITn−1, ES = −eishaft + Δ9), computed here only at grades 6–8 — grade 5 would need an IT4 value this page doesn't tabulate, so K5/M5/N5 stay unsupported rather than guess; switch the hole letter to H to check the shaft side of an unsupported combination instead. Tick "enter manually" on either side to type its deviations directly, in µm from nominal size — upper (ES/es) and lower (EI/ei), positive above nominal and negative below — for a part with a known but non-standard tolerance (an off-the-shelf component, a supplier datasheet, a drawing callout that doesn't match an ISO 286 letter/grade). The two sides are independent: e.g. tick it for the shaft only to check a manufacturer's shaft (say "+0/−0.013 mm", i.e. es = 0, ei = −13) against a standard hole picked normally above.

Per Equipmake's dowel guidance7: dowels are ISO 2388, in m6 or h8 grade; m6 is the more widely available and the default for new designs.

Applicable design standards

  • ISO 286-1:2010 — Geometrical product specifications (GPS) — ISO code system for tolerances on linear sizes — Part 1: Basis of tolerances, deviations and fits.
  • ISO 286-2:2010 — Geometrical product specifications (GPS) — ISO code system for tolerances on linear sizes — Part 2: Tables of standard tolerance classes and limit deviations for holes and shafts.
  • DIN 7190-1:2017 — Interference fits — Part 1: Calculation and design rules for cylindrical single joints (governs the press/shrink-fit design in section 3, beyond what ISO 286 itself covers).

This page computes limits from the standards' own formulae and fundamental-deviation tables rather than reproducing their tolerance-class tables verbatim; conformance-critical work should refer to the standards themselves.
