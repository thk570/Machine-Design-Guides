4. O-ring Size Range — Thermal Fit

Suggests a free size range that keeps the installed fit within limits across your temperature range, since the elastomer expands faster than the metal5. Mode (stretch/compression), mating diameter and limit all follow the seal type selected in section 3.

Applicable design standards

  • ISO 3601-1 — Fluid power systems — O-rings — Part 1: Inside diameters, cross-sections, tolerances and designation codes
  • ISO 3601-2 — Fluid power systems — O-rings — Part 2: Housing dimensions for general applications (groove/gland design)
  • ISO 3601-3 — Fluid power systems — O-rings — Part 3: Quality acceptance criteria
  • ISO 3601-4 — Fluid power systems — O-rings — Part 4: Anti-extrusion rings (back-up rings)
  • ISO 1 — Geometrical product specifications (GPS) — Standard reference temperature for the specification of geometrical and dimensional properties (the 20°C basis used in section 4)
  • ASTM D2240 — Standard Test Method for Rubber Property — Durometer Hardness (the Shore A scale used in section 1)
  • ASTM D2000 — Standard Classification System for Rubber Products in Automotive Applications (elastomer compound classification)

This page doesn't reproduce these standards' own tables/codes — check the actual standard for a design that must claim conformance.
